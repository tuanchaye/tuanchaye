When the weather is hot, few things beat a glass of cold brew tea. The best part? It is almost impossible to get wrong, and it needs no kettle, no thermometer, and no timing skill.

## Why Cold Brew?
Brewing tea in cold water extracts flavor slowly. The result is:

- **Smoother and sweeter** — less bitterness and astringency
- **Lower in caffeine** — cold water pulls less caffeine than hot
- **Naturally refreshing** — perfect over ice

## The Basic Method
1. Add **about 5–8 g of tea** to **500 ml of cold water** (a jar or bottle works).
2. Put it in the **refrigerator**.
3. Wait **6–12 hours** (overnight is ideal).
4. Strain and enjoy. It keeps well in the fridge for 2–3 days.

## Best Teas for Cold Brew
- **Green tea:** light, grassy, clean
- **White tea:** naturally sweet, almost like water with a hint of melon
- **Oolong:** floral and fragrant when cold
- **Fruit-forward black teas:** bold but smooth

Avoid heavily roasted or smoky teas, which can taste flat when cold.

## Quick Tips
- Use filtered water for the cleanest taste.
- A reusable tea bag or infuser ball makes cleanup effortless.
- For a stronger cup, use more leaf rather than longer time.

Cold brew is the friendliest way to fall in love with tea — set it up before bed, and breakfast comes with a ready-made, delicious drink.
