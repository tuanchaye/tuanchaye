Gongfu Cha (工夫茶), literally "making tea with skill," is a Chinese brewing
tradition that uses a small teapot or gaiwan, a high leaf-to-water ratio, and
many short infusions to reveal a tea's full character.

## The Essentials

- **Gaiwan or small clay pot** — keeps leaf ratio high
- **Fairness pitcher (cha hai)** — evens the strength across cups
- **Small tasting cups** — sip and savor
- **A tea tray** — catches rinse water

## The Rhythm

1. Rinse the leaves quickly with hot water (awakens the tea, discarded).
2. Brew for 5–15 seconds for early infusions; lengthen over time.
3. Pour into the fairness pitcher, then divide into cups.

## Why It Matters

Gongfu Cha is less about formality and more about attention — to water,
time and the leaf itself. It is the most rewarding way to experience fine
Chinese tea.
